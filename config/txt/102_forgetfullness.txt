Babuji: Birthe, my sister Birthe, I think, was greatly benefitted in a few days here? Abhyasi: Oh yes, oh yes.
Babuji: As she grows, or you grow, you will feel yourself better and better. Abhyasi: Yes.
Babuji: Only proper inclination should be there. It should not be disturbed in any way.
Inclination should not be disturbed.
Abhyasi: No. She remained very forgetful. Babuji: Hm ?
Abhyasi: She remained very forgetful, Birthe for some time after returning. She was very forgetful, which was good.
Babuji: Gradually, it comes sometimes for some time, one day, two days. Sometimes one hour, two hours, and when it becomes permanent, of course, it is a state. Divine state.
Abhyasi: Yes
Babuji: Forgetful state means, you do not remember that you exist. In other words, all your body, except, or soul; nothing of the sort.
     And I am telling you, if I describe my condition, of course I cannot describe because words are not there. No taste. Tasteless taste. I say, tasteless taste. How to describe it?
Let me see the pulse of hookah.
