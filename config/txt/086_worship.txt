Babuji: Of course there may be some other sort of worships, in different countries also, method may differ. The methods must be simple, first thing; and purification is the work of the master, and the taught also that should be done by both - then you shine out. We generally look towards our physical body we do not look towards the astral body, which is our own. That is the other defect. We take care of this body, or this personality, but we do not pay attention to the Inner, which is a real. Being, hidden in some corner of our thought. We have not to seek Him, we have not to seek Him; He Himself seeks you. Provided you are prepared for it, you will feel. You will feel some stone ?You will not feel any stone lying on the street which is fit for the building. If you are fit for that edifice, then of course everything comes to you - so try to be fit!
