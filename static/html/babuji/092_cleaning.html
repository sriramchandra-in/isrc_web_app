Abhyasi: After establishing connection with Master, some abhyasis feel that there is no necessity of a preceptor. How do you say Master?

Babuji: If they are absorbed in the Master no meditation nothing is necessary, - if totally absorbed, no meditation, nothing of the sort. But unless they are absorbed, and if the Master is of that type, who deserves, that people may absorb in Him, that should be the condition. Then no meditation, nothing of the sort is needed.
