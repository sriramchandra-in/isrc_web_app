Babuji: If they are useful you may do it.

Abhyasi: I don't know if they are useful till I get home. They seem to be nice to read now though too.

Abhyasi: Thomas has said to me, sometimes, we can hear something and not understand it till 2 or 3 month later.

Babuji: You hear something and not understand it ?? Abhyasi: 2 or 3 month later.

Abhyasi: Sometimes you will say something and we do not understand it, now, but later on.

Babuji: It happens. As you improve you will understand, because, you see, I talk with the heart and not with the tongue.

And so many persons have said, not only you.

All given in it is my own experience. All experience! I did not try to borrow others ideas. And moreover I have not read much so.

Abhyasi: You have not what? Babuji: I have not read much.

     If I would have read much of course, Milton, Shakespeare, Wordsworth, Wordsworth. All these ideas. I would, the ? said so, Wordsworth said so ? but I have not read, what to do? I want to write a book.

My Master was a well-read person. Of course I feel that I have done wrong that I did not read the books.

I feel deficiency in a way. I would have given better reply, had I been done that.

Abhyasi: I don't think so. I have read a few of those books.
