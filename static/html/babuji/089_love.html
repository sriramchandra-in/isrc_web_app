Abhyasi: Yesterday you were talking about love. What is love?

Babuji: Opening yourself to Reality or Divinity is love. When we are open, in a way, the creation is a whim. And I have opened everything to Him. There is nothing between myself and God. I don't know whether

I spoke this word correctly.

Abhyasi: Yes, then only a real master can give real love all the time. Is that correct? Babuji: Well, how to love, we should ask this question, "How to love?"

By remembrance. If you love anybody you will remember. And if you begin to remember Him, He will begin to love you. That is all . . . .

Abhyasi: Supposing one doesn't know how to love? Can he take up to meditation and attain the End?

Babuji: And he takes the meditation? Of course, meditation, if he is interested in meditation, will itself create love. And if he is not interested, no. Of course by transmission light goes, that nobody can ... even if you give to a dacoit, the light will go.

Abhyasi: Now then Master, we have so many gurus and so many abhyasis taking up to yoga.

Are there any categories among gurus and abhyasis?

Babuji: Well, if divine qualities are there in guru, he can be selected. The other qualities is that he can transmit his own force. And Swami Vivekananda, he also said, "There are gurus who can lead me to perfection, but I want such a guru who may utilize his own power for our perfection". That guru I will take. Because time is saved. But you should be interested.
